# School Dynamics — Marketing video script

**Format:** Voiceover + screen recording (optional B-roll noted below)  
**Approx. length:** ~2–2½ minutes at a calm read (adjust pauses on site)  
**Tone:** Confident, clear, warm — schools and parents, not engineers  

---

## SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN  
**~0:00 – 0:20**

**VISUAL**  
Quick montage: busy school office, papers on a desk, phone ringing — *or* skip B-roll and open straight on the School Dynamics logo and product UI.

**NARRATOR**  
Running a school means juggling a thousand details. Fees in one place. Attendance in another. Grades somewhere else. And parents still need answers.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT (optional)**  
*One school. Too many disconnected tools.*

**NARRATOR (beat)**  
School Dynamics brings it together — with one front door for staff, and another built just for parents.

---

## SCENE 2 — THE LANDING PAGE  
**~0:20 – 0:40**

**VISUAL**  
Full screen: the School Dynamics landing page. Slow pan or zoom on the two portal cards — Staff and Parent.

**NARRATOR**  
When you open School Dynamics, you’re not thrown into a generic login. You choose your path.

**VISUAL**  
Highlight “Staff portal” card — then “Parent portal” card.

**NARRATOR**  
Administrators and teachers enter the **staff portal** — the full workspace to run the school.  
Parents and guardians enter the **parent portal** — a clear view of what matters to their family.

**NARRATOR (closing line)**  
Two doors. One school. Everyone lands where they belong.

---

## SCENE 3 — STAFF PORTAL: SIGN IN & DASHBOARD  
**~0:40 – 1:05**

**VISUAL**  
Staff sign-in screen → enter email → transition to dashboard.

**NARRATOR**  
Staff sign in with a school email and step straight into the **dashboard** — your morning briefing.

**VISUAL**  
Pan across stat cards: active students, present today, fees outstanding, exam records. Scroll to “Attendance today” and “Recent results” if visible.

**NARRATOR**  
How many learners are active. Who’s present today. What fees are still outstanding. How exam records are building up — with shortcuts to go deeper when you’re ready.

**NARRATOR**  
One screen. A sense of the whole day — before you open a single drawer.

---

## SCENE 4 — STAFF: STUDENTS, FEES, ATTENDANCE  
**~1:05 – 1:40**

**VISUAL**  
**Students** — table of learners; quick click **Add student** or open one student’s profile showing guardian, fees summary, results.

**NARRATOR**  
The **student registry** holds admission details, class, stream, and guardian contacts. Add new learners anytime — and open any child to see their fees and grades in context.

**VISUAL**  
**Fees** — invoice table in UGX; **Add invoice** flow (student, term, amount, paid, due date).

**NARRATOR**  
**Fees** aren’t buried in a spreadsheet. Every invoice is tied to a student — amount, what’s paid, balance, due date, and status — so the money story stays clear.

**VISUAL**  
**Attendance** — pick a date → **Build register** → flip present/absent on rows.

**NARRATOR**  
**Attendance** works the way a real school day does. Pick the date. Build the register for every active student. Mark present or absent — fast, consistent, and easy to find later.

---

## SCENE 5 — STAFF: EXAMS & MESSAGES  
**~1:40 – 2:00**

**VISUAL**  
**Exams** — gradebook table → **Add result** (student, subject, term, score).

**NARRATOR**  
**Exams** live beside attendance and fees — not on a separate island. Record scores by subject and term; grades follow from the marks you enter.

**VISUAL**  
**Messages** — list of communications → **Log message** (from, channel, summary).

**NARRATOR**  
**Messages** create a trail — internal notes, SMS, WhatsApp, email — who said what, and when. So communication with families stays accountable.

---

## SCENE 6 — PARENT PORTAL  
**~2:00 – 2:35**

**VISUAL**  
Log out or cut to parent device. Landing page again → **Parent sign in** → parent login field (admission or phone).

**NARRATOR**  
Parents don’t need staff credentials. They sign in with what the school already knows — an admission number, or the phone number on file.

**VISUAL**  
Parent **home** — learner cards, balances, last attendance; then **Fees**, **Attendance**, **Grades** in short clips.

**NARRATOR**  
On the **parent home**, they see each child at a glance — class, fee balance, and the latest attendance.

**NARRATOR**  
Open **fees** for invoices and balances. **Attendance** for a clear history — day by day. **Grades** for published results — subject by subject, term by term.

**NARRATOR**  
They see *their* children — not the whole school. Trust built in.

---

## SCENE 7 — CLOSING  
**~2:35 – 2:50**

**VISUAL**  
Split or quick crossfade: staff dashboard ↔ parent home. End on logo + school name or tagline card.

**NARRATOR**  
Staff run the school from one workspace. Parents stay informed through a portal built for them.

**NARRATOR (tagline)**  
**School Dynamics** — school management that keeps everyone on the same page.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT (optional)**  
School Dynamics  
*Staff portal · Parent portal · One platform*

**FADE OUT.**

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES (not voiced)

- **Pacing:** Leave 1–2 seconds on key UI actions (clicks, page loads) before the next narration line.  
- **Roles:** Use separate browser profiles or sign out between staff and parent segments so the audience follows the switch.  
- **Consistency:** Same school name and 2–3 sample students across scenes so parent matching looks real.  
- **Music:** Light, modern, African-inspired or neutral corporate — duck under narration; avoid competing with the voice.  
- **Length variants:** For a 60-second cut, use Scenes 1–2, compress 3–4 into one dashboard + one feature (fees *or* attendance), Scene 6 shortened to parent home + one tab, Scene 7.

---

*Script aligned with the School Dynamics web app: landing page, staff portal, and parent portal. For a live product film, authentication and hosting may differ; the story above still applies.*
